# Airframe #1 — 2026-11-21 photo session

These photographs document the FC-over-ESC stack, upper carrier, adjustable camera mount,
landing legs, GPS mast and underslung battery tray at that session. Some frames were taken
with the compute board temporarily removed for inspection. No powered operation is implied
by the photographs.

Later corrections, including the current mounting description, supplied part files,
compute-board identification and H1–H4 clearances, are centralized in the
[current as-built status](../../docs/as-built-status.md). This photo set does not confirm
