# Fasteners by joint

Sizes and counts follow the assembly tutorial and hardware registry. Screw lengths are measured along the shank, excluding the head.

| Joint | Screws / rods | Spacers, standoffs, washers or grommets | Nuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor feet, arms and motor bases | 8 × M3×16, inserted upward (2 per foot) | — | — |
| Battery tray to frame, inner 20×20 mm pattern | 4 × M3×20, inserted upward from below the tray | — | — |
| Outer center-stack rods through tray and frame | 4 × M3×40, inserted upward from below the tray | — | — |
| ESC lower support on outer rods | Uses the 4 outer M3×40 rods | 4 × M3×6×1.5 silicone washers, one below the ESC at each rod | 4 × M3 nuts, 2.5 mm thick, below the washers |
| ESC upper support, below adapter plate | Uses the 4 outer M3×40 rods | 8 × M3×6×1.5 silicone washers, two above the ESC at each rod | 4 × M3 nuts, 5 mm thick |
| Adapter plate on outer rods | Uses the 4 outer M3×40 rods | 4 silicone damping grommets in the adapter plate's outer holes; each has a Ø4×1.5 mm waist and Ø6 mm flanges | 2 × M3 nuts, 2.5 mm thick, on the left-side (+X) rods only, above the grommet flanges |
| Flight controller to adapter plate, inner 20×20 mm pattern | 4 × M3×12, inserted downward | 4 × M3×6×1.5 silicone washers on top of the flight controller, under the screw heads | 4 × M3 nuts, 2.5 mm thick, below the adapter plate |
| Carrier plate on outer rods | Uses the 4 outer M3×40 rods | 4 × M3 single-ended hex standoffs; 5 mm body, 4 mm blind bore fitted over each rod end, 6 mm upper rod | 4 × M3 nuts, 2.5 mm thick, above the carrier plate |
| IO board to carrier plate | 4 × M2.5×16, inserted upward | 4 × M2×5×1 silicone washers below the carrier plate and 4 above the standoffs; each washer's modeled Ø2 mm bore is expanded to 2.5 mm. 4 × M2.5 hex through-hole standoffs, 6 mm high | 4 × M2.5 thin nuts, 2 mm thick, above the IO board |
| GPS mast to carrier plate | 2 × M3×12, inserted downward | 2 × M3×7×1 hard plastic washers above the mast base | 2 × M3 nuts, 2.5 mm thick, below the carrier plate |
| Camera board / bracket | — | 2 white spacer sleeves | — |

The adapter's four outer-hole grommets are vibration mounts for the outer M3×40 rods. The four FC washers are separate M3 silicone washers at the inner mounting holes.
